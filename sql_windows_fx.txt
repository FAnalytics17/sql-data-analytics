they allow for calculating running totals or finding maximum group values e.g highest paid employee(max) per department(grouped)
they conduct operations across related rows , aggregate fx summarizes single column or columns

types of window functions
I.Aggregate
  AVG()  MIN()  MAX()   SUM()  COUNT()

II. Ranking
  ROW_NUMBER - ranks unique rows (i.e 1,2,3,4....n)
  RANK ties are skipped 1,2,2,4,4,4,7,8
  DENSE_RANK rank sequentially and keeps numeric order i.e 1,2,2,3,3,3,4,5 where, 2 items tie and are given number 2 and the next is given number 3

III. Analytical
  LAG()  LEAD()  FIRST_VALUE()  LAST_VALUE()
